Live Catalogue and Posters

With a free TMDB key, the Library, search and Deck draw on TMDB's whole catalogue with real posters. This only works when the app runs on its own web address. Inside Claude, outside requests are blocked, so the built-in titles and drawn art stay.

Open the setup link once on another device and it saves your keys there. Keep the link to yourself, because it contains your keys.

Live IMDb Scores

TMDB does not carry IMDb scores. With a free OMDb key (omdbapi.com, 1,000 lookups a day), the card on top of the Deck and any title you open show the current IMDb score. Without it, live titles show TMDB's own score, labelled TMDB.
